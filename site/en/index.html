Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Where is Noémia located?

Noémia is located at R. do Duque, 21, 1200-158 Lisbon.

What are Noémia's opening hours?

Noémia is open Thursday from 6pm to 11pm, and Friday to Monday from 7pm to 11pm. Closed on Tuesday and Wednesday.

Is there live fado every night?

There are live fado performances every 30 minutes, Friday to Monday, from 8pm to 11pm.

Do I need to book a table?

We recommend booking in advance, especially on fado nights, through our online booking system or by phone.

What is Noémia's price range?

Noémia has a moderate price range (€€), with tapas to share and Portuguese wines by the glass or bottle.
